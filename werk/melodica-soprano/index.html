Melodica Soprano

15 December 2025

sound art

composition

This piece was first presented in a quadraphonic setup during VRT Radio 1’s ‘Nacht van het Luisteren’ (Night of Listening) at STUK in Leuven. What you can listen to here is a stereo downmix. I made the piece during a residency week at STUK, under the guidance of Aurélie Nyirabikali Lierman and Jeroen Vandesande.

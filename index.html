Sebastiaan Distelmans (Bas) is an audio maker and musician based in Brussels. In 2026 he earned a master of arts in radio at RITCS, and his master's thesis The Ordeals of a Certain Arno Schaak was selected for broadcast on Zandman (VRT Radio 1). During his internship he worked on the podcast Alpha by Wederik De Backer and Lieselot Mariën, published by Knack and NTR. Together with Wederik he also made The Guards of Gelmel Castle: a documentary podcast series about daily life in Hoogstraten prison (PSC Hoogstraten).

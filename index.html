I'm Sebastiaan Distelmans (Bas), an audio maker. In 2026 I earned a Master of Arts in radio at RITCS, and my master's thesis The Ordeals of a Certain Arno Schaak was selected for broadcast on Zandman (VRT Radio 1). During my internship I worked on the podcast Alpha by Wederik De Backer and Lieselot Mariën, and together with Wederik I made The Guards of Gelmel Castle: a documentary podcast series about life in Hoogstraten prison (PSC Hoogstraten).
